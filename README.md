# bulwer-lytton-contest-data

JSON-formatted files of the winners of the [Bulwer-Lytton Fiction Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulwer-Lytton_Fiction_Contest) for the worst possible opening sentences to novels never written. Compiled from https://www.bulwer-lytton.com/ and https://archive.org/

As an aside, I received a "Dishonorable Mention" in 2023's Odious Outliers category for my submission:

"The lazy summer afternoon slowly turned to evening, and no one at the Stillforest Town Potluck took note when he picked up the first one, nor the next one or the one after that, but for weeks folks in the sleepy community would excitedly talk about nothing else but the time they saw Hank McGillie carry seven folding chairs at once."


## Errata

Not including simple typos, the following errors were found while reviewing the data:

* The 2010 Romance category Runner Up and Dishonorable Mention entries are the same. The Dishonorable Mention entry was removed.

* The 2010 through TODO Odious Outliers category doesn't have a winner or dishonorable mention entry. I don't know if this was intentional or not.
